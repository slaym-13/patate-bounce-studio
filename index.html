Patate Bounce Studio

Give every patate its own PNG and its own sound. Merge 1 through 9, record, post.

Recording grabs the stage plus the live audio mix. It stops on its own after 90 seconds.

The patates

Each row is one patate. PNG sets its look — by default it is centre-cropped and masked to a circle, so square images come out round; untick the box above to keep the original shape and transparency. Sound for patate 1 fires every time it hits the ring; for 2–9 it fires the moment that patate is formed. Only one patate sound is ever audible — whichever plays last cuts off the one still playing. The exception is a reveal: the first time a level is ever reached, its clip is protected and plays out in full, and nothing interrupts it. A lower patate also will not talk over a higher one that is still playing: it is muted instead, 60% of the time by default. The odds slider on each row sets how often that level actually makes its sound; it starts on a curve where low numbers speak less, since they bounce and merge constantly. A reveal ignores the odds and always plays. The size slider next to it nudges that one level up or down from the 25% ladder, and resizes any already in the ring. Empty 2–9 slots fall back to a chime that rises with the number; patate 1 stays silent until you give it a clip.

Bounce sound

    Bounce level is deliberately near the floor — the ring hits should sit under the patate sounds, not compete with them. Drag it to 0 to silence them completely.

    Merging

    Two patates of the same number become one of the next number, 25% bigger, up to the top level. Levels sets how many there are — raising it adds rows to the patate list and keeps the files you already picked. New 1s fall in through the chute at the top.
